# Mock interviews

**PrepAI's mock interviews are timed practice sessions that simulate a real technical interview, available in three formats — AI-led, peer-led, and mentor-led — each with optional recording, structured feedback, and per-session scoring.** They let you rehearse the *performance* of interviewing, not just the problem-solving.

## The three formats

| Format | Who runs it | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-led** | An AI interviewer powered by Google Gemini's free tier | Practising any time, instantly, without scheduling. |
| **Peer-led** | Another PrepAI user | Realistic back-and-forth and learning by interviewing others. |
| **Mentor-led** | A user with the mentor role | Higher-signal feedback from someone more experienced. |

## How an AI mock interview works

1. You start a session and choose a focus (for example, a topic or difficulty).
2. The AI interviewer asks questions and follow-ups, adapting to your answers.
3. You respond in the session UI; the interview is **timed** to mirror real conditions.
4. At the end you receive **structured feedback** and a **score** for the session.

Because the AI uses a language model, treat its scoring as **useful guidance, not a final verdict** — it is excellent for spotting gaps and rehearsing articulation, but a human interviewer weighs things a model can't. See [Configure a Gemini API key](../guides/configure-gemini-key.md) if you are self-hosting and want to supply your own key.

## Peer and mentor interviews

Peer and mentor sessions pair you with another person in a live session. These add the human dimension the AI can't fully replace: communication, collaboration under pressure, and judgement. Mentor-led sessions are run by users with the mentor role and are aimed at higher-signal feedback.

## Recording and feedback

Sessions can optionally be **recorded** so you can review how you communicated, where you hesitated, and how you structured your answer. Feedback is captured in a structured form rather than a freeform note, which makes it easier to compare sessions over time.

## Scoring over time

Each session produces a score, and those scores feed your [dashboard](./dashboard-analytics.md) so you can see whether your interview performance is trending up as you practise. Like all PrepAI metrics, scores are a tool for reflection — the goal is steady improvement, not a single number.

## Getting the most out of mock interviews

- **Think out loud.** Interviewers grade communication as much as correctness; rehearse narrating your approach.
- **Do AI reps daily, human reps weekly.** AI sessions are unlimited and instant; peer/mentor sessions are higher-signal but need scheduling.
- **Review your recordings.** Watching yourself is uncomfortable and extremely effective.
- **Pair with practice drills.** Drill the topic in [coding practice](./coding-practice.md) first, then rehearse explaining it live.

## FAQ

**Is the AI interviewer free?** Yes — it uses Google Gemini's free tier on the hosted app. Self-hosters supply their own free Gemini key.

**Can the AI replace a real interviewer?** No, and PrepAI doesn't claim it can. It's a rehearsal partner that's always available; peer and mentor sessions add the human signal.

**Are sessions recorded by default?** Recording is optional and under your control.

**How is my score calculated?** The AI evaluates your answers against the session's rubric. Use it as directional feedback, not a pass/fail.
