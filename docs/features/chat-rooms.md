# Chat rooms

**PrepAI's chat rooms are real-time spaces — public or private — where you can discuss problems, coordinate peer mock interviews, and study with others.** Interview prep is less lonely and more effective with people, and chat rooms are where that collaboration happens.

## Public and private rooms

- **Public rooms** are open study spaces organised by topic or category that anyone can join.
- **Private rooms** are invite-based, ideal for a study group or a pair preparing together. You can create a room and optionally set an invite code (or let one be auto-generated).

## Real-time messaging

Messages are delivered in real time, so a room feels like a live conversation rather than a slow forum. You can search rooms, filter by category, and jump into the one that fits what you're working on.

## What people use rooms for

- **Working through a hard drill** together when stuck.
- **Pairing for [peer mock interviews](./mock-interviews.md)** — agree on a time and format in the room.
- **Sharing resources** and discussing approaches to common interview topics.
- **Accountability** — a study group keeps your [streak](./dashboard-analytics.md) alive.

## Creating a room

1. Open the chat section and choose to create a room.
2. Give it a name and topic, pick a category, and add a short description.
3. Choose public or private; for private rooms, set a member limit and (optionally) an invite code.
4. Share the room with the people you want in it.

## Etiquette

- Keep public rooms on-topic and welcoming to newcomers.
- Use private rooms for focused group prep.
- Treat the room as you would a real study group — be helpful, be specific, and reciprocate.

## FAQ

**Are chat rooms free?** Yes, they're part of the standard experience.

**Can I limit who joins?** Yes — create a private room with an invite code and a member cap.

**Is chat real-time?** Yes, messages are delivered live so conversations feel natural.
