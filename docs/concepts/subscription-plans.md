# Subscription plans

**PrepAI offers three plans — Free, Pro, and Ultimate — where the core experience (practice, mock interviews, peer reviews, analytics, and tools) is free, and the paid tiers add convenience features like unlimited downloads and richer export bundles.** You never need to pay to prepare for an interview with PrepAI.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Price | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Unlimited topic viewing and in-browser runs; 50 ZIP downloads per day; solution reveal after one attempt. |
| **Pro** | $8 / month | Unlimited downloads; ZIP includes the reference solution; private history. |
| **Ultimate** | $14 / month | Everything in Pro, plus ZIP bundles that include all guides as Markdown. |

The differences are **conveniences**, not gates on learning — every drill, mock interview, review, and tool is available on the free plan.

## How billing works

PayPal subscriptions are the planned billing integration. Today, the `/checkout` page routes to the maintainer's payment page with the chosen plan, and an administrator sets your plan on your account. This honest, stubbed flow exists because PrepAI follows a strict no-paid-third-party-billing-SDK policy in line with its [zero-cost architecture](./zero-cost-architecture.md).

There's also a one-time **support/contribution** link on the pricing page for people who want to back the project without a subscription.

## Why a free core matters

PrepAI's whole premise is that interview preparation shouldn't be locked behind a paywall. Charging only for conveniences (bulk downloads, richer export bundles, private history) keeps the playing field level: someone with no budget gets the same drills, the same AI interviewer, and the same reviews as a paying user.

## Which plan should you pick?

- **Free** is the right default for almost everyone — start here.
- **Pro** makes sense if you download solution bundles a lot and want private history.
- **Ultimate** is for power users who want every guide bundled as Markdown for offline study.

## FAQ

**Can I prepare fully on the free plan?** Yes — all drills, mock interviews, reviews, analytics, and tools are free.

**What do paid plans add?** Download conveniences, reference solutions in ZIPs, private history, and (Ultimate) guide bundles.

**How is billing handled?** PayPal subscriptions are planned; today checkout routes to a payment page and plans are applied to your account. There's also a one-time support link.
