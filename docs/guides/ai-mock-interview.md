# Run an AI mock interview

**This guide shows you how to run an AI-led mock interview in PrepAI and turn its feedback into real improvement.** The AI interviewer is powered by Google Gemini's free tier and is available any time, with no scheduling.

## Steps

1. **Open mock interviews.** Go to the interviews section (`/interviews`).
2. **Start an AI session.** Choose the AI-led format.
3. **Pick a focus.** Select a topic and/or difficulty so questions match what you want to practise.
4. **Begin.** The session is timed to mirror a real interview.
5. **Answer the questions.** The AI asks questions and adaptive follow-ups based on your responses.
6. **Think out loud.** Narrate your approach as you would with a human interviewer — communication is half the grade.
7. **Finish.** You receive structured feedback and a session score.
8. **Review and log.** Your score feeds the [dashboard](../features/dashboard-analytics.md) so you can track the trend.

## Getting useful feedback

- **Be specific in your answers.** Vague answers get vague feedback; concrete reasoning gets concrete critique.
- **Treat the score as guidance.** The AI is excellent at spotting gaps and rehearsing articulation, but it's a language model — look at the trend across sessions, not a single number.
- **Re-run with the same focus.** Repeating a focus area shows whether the feedback stuck.

## When to use AI vs human sessions

| Use AI sessions when… | Use peer/mentor sessions when… |
|---|---|
| You want unlimited, instant reps | You want higher-signal, human feedback |
| You're rehearsing articulation and structure | You're practising collaboration and communication under real back-and-forth |
| It's late and nobody's available | You can schedule with a peer or mentor |

Do AI reps daily and human reps weekly for the best of both.
