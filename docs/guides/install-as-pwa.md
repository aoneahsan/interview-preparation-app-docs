# Install PrepAI as a PWA

**PrepAI is a Progressive Web App, so you can install it from the browser to get an app icon, a standalone window without browser chrome, and offline access to content you've already loaded.** It works on desktop, Android, and iOS.

## Why install the PWA

- **One-tap launch** from your home screen or dock.
- **Standalone window** — no address bar, more room for the app.
- **Offline resilience** — already-loaded drills and tools keep working without a connection (sign-in, sync, and AI features still need the network).

## Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

1. Open [interviewpai.aoneahsan.com](https://interviewpai.aoneahsan.com).
2. Click the **install icon** in the address bar, **or** open the browser menu and choose **Install PrepAI**.
3. Confirm. PrepAI opens in its own window and appears in your apps list.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the app in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu (**⋮**).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** / **Install app** and confirm.

> Prefer a store install? PrepAI is also on **[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aoneahsan.interviewpai)** as a native Android app.

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open the app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen** and confirm.

This gives iOS users a near-native, full-screen experience while the App Store build is pending.

## Updating

The PWA updates itself when you reload with a connection. PrepAI shows an update prompt when a new version is available so you can refresh at a good time.

## FAQ

**Does the PWA work offline?** Already-loaded content does. Sign-in, syncing, mock interviews, and reviews need a connection.

**Is the PWA different from the Play Store app?** They share the same experience; the Play Store build adds native niceties (splash screen, status-bar theming, native share, in-app review).

**How do I uninstall?** Remove it like any app — from your home screen on mobile, or your OS apps list on desktop.
