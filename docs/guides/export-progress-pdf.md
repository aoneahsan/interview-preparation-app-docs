# Export your progress as a PDF

**This guide shows how to export your PrepAI dashboard as a PDF you can share with a mentor or recruiter — generated client-side, so your data isn't sent anywhere to produce the file.**

## When this is useful

- Showing a **mentor** what you've covered so they can coach the gaps.
- Giving a **recruiter** evidence that you've prepared systematically.
- Keeping a personal **record** of milestones and streaks over time.

## Steps

1. **Open the dashboard.** Sign in and go to your dashboard (`/dashboard`).
2. **Let it load.** Wait for your charts (streaks, per-track completion, milestones, scores) to render.
3. **Choose export.** Use the dashboard's export action to generate a PDF.
4. **Save or share.** The PDF downloads to your device; attach it to an email or message.

## What's in the PDF

The export captures the same picture you see on screen — your practice streak, per-track completion, milestones, and mock-interview score trend — laid out for printing and sharing.

## Good practice

- **Export before a real interview** as a study summary and a confidence boost.
- **Re-export periodically** to see the change over weeks, not days.
- **Pair it with specifics.** A PDF plus a sentence ("I focused on Node.js and microservices this month") tells a clear story.

## Privacy

The PDF is built **in your browser**. Producing it doesn't upload your data anywhere — consistent with PrepAI's [privacy model](../concepts/privacy-and-data.md), where your code runs locally and your analytics belong to your account.

## FAQ

**Does exporting cost anything?** No — it's part of the free experience.

**Is my data uploaded to make the PDF?** No, generation happens on your device.
